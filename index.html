        Help & settings

        How to use it

        • Open a PDF, PowerPoint (.pptx) or Word (.docx) file, or drop one anywhere on the page. Each file becomes its own notebook in the sidebar; Insert › Add a file here adds one to the notebook you're in instead.
        • PowerPoint files open as the slides look: each slide is a page with its text you can select, and you write between the lines as on a PDF. Complex animations and SmartArt may still differ; for an exact copy, save the slides as a PDF (File › Save as › PDF) and open that. Choose Editable text under Open PowerPoint as (View tab, or below) to rebuild the slides as text you can type inside instead.
        • Word files keep their fonts, colours, tables, headers and pictures as editable text. A few pictures (old EMF or WMF drawings, for example) may not come through: save the file as a PDF and open that, or add the picture with Insert › Picture, video or sound.
        • On a PDF page, point at a spot and press + Add here to add a note there (the page opens up between the lines). Right-click the button (press and hold on a touch screen) to pick Note, Sticky note or Equation. On other pages, click where you want to write. In a note, type / for the same choices.
        • Writing tools (Insert tab): Writing guide puts a grid, ruled lines or dots over the pages so you can write straight with a pen or Apple Pencil. Drag it by its handle, pull its edges to resize it, and press Esc or × to take it away; it is never saved in your notebook. Its bar goes when you click elsewhere: point at the faint icon in its top left corner to bring it back, or point at the Insert button for its settings. Table opens a grid to choose the size, then drop it in a note: Tab moves between cells, drag the borders to size it, and right-click (or ⋯) for rows, columns and merging. Journal entry is an accounting entry, lined up in columns (also under + Add here and by typing /journal): click DR or CR to switch a row, Enter adds a row, Tab and the arrow keys move around like a table, and right-click (or the × when you point at it) deletes the entry.
        • Sticky notes sit on top of the page: drag one by its tape to move it, even onto another page. In the Notes panel, type a note and press Add note, or drag Sticky onto any page.
        • Draw (Insert tab) writes or highlights on PDF pages with a pen, and the eraser rubs strokes out. Select words on any page to highlight them, or press Comment; open comments are listed in the Notes panel until you resolve them.
        • The Home tab styles your notes: the font, size and colour are used for new notes, or click in a note (or select words) to change just that.
        • Point at a note (or tap it) and drag any edge or corner to resize it: the opposite side stays where it is. Drag its move handle to move it. Pink lines show when it lines up with other notes; hold Alt or Shift to move it freely.
        • New notes line up with the note above or below them when they are close (Box › Auto-align notes). To tidy up, Select all on the Box tab, then use Align: left, centre or right, Same width, Distribute (floating and sticky notes, evenly spaced) or Tidy page. Each is one step you can take Back.
        • View › Side pages gives each slide a page for your own notes beside it. They work in the Default, A4 and A3 layouts (each printed page gets one beside it, and both shrink together when the window is narrow). Point near the top right corner of a side page (or tap it, or press Tab) to show a palette button that changes its paper, or use Side page background in the View tab. Slides, side pages and notes are scaled to fit the window as one, so nothing moves when you open the sidebar.
        • On each note: the arrows float it on top of the page, the minus minimises it to a pencil icon (drag the icon aside, right-click it to colour it), and the pin keeps it open.
        • The sidebar (left) lists your notebooks: click one to switch, and the one you're in shows its pages. Use its ⋯ to rename or delete it. Hide it with « (Ctrl+\) and point at the left edge to peek at it. A notebook’s ⋯ menu can also star it as a Favourite, Duplicate it, or move it to the Trash, where it waits 30 days (and can be restored) before it is gone for ever. Group notebooks in folders (New folder): drag a notebook or a folder onto a folder to move it, or use Move to… in its ⋯ menu.
        • Notes (right) lists every note in the notebook to search, filter by icon colour and jump to.
        • Install as an app (below, or at the bottom of the sidebar) gives Between the Lines its own window, or a Home Screen icon on an iPad or iPhone, and it works offline.
        • Focus hides the toolbar, side panels and status bar so only your pages and notes are left (Esc brings them back). Each panel also closes with its ×, and View › Toolbar can shrink the toolbar to tabs only.
        • Study mode stops accidental changes while you revise. Everything is saved in this browser; Download a PDF, or a notebook file to keep editing on another device.

        On iPad and tablets

        • Add to Home Screen: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Between the Lines then opens full screen like an app, and keeps working offline (How to install below shows the steps).
        • Apple Pencil (and other pens) draws on a PDF page or a side page with the tool from Draw, even when Draw is off. Pressing harder makes the line thicker, and the Pencil’s eraser end (where it has one) rubs strokes out. Your fingers still scroll, tap and select, and a hand resting on the screen while you write is ignored. A quick tap with the Pencil works like a tap: it shows + Add here. To draw with a finger, turn on Draw.
        • Scribble: tap inside a note and write with the Pencil; iPadOS turns your handwriting into text (switch it on in Settings › Apple Pencil). The Pencil never draws on top of a note, so you can always write in one.
        • Press and hold does what right-click does: on + Add here (sticky note or equation), on a pencil icon’s colours, on a misspelt word (suggestions) and on a notebook in the sidebar. Tap a note to show its handles for moving and resizing.
        • Tap with two fingers on the page to go Back one step (undo).
        • Held upright, the sidebar and the Notes list open over the pages, so the pages keep their size: tap outside to close them.

        Install as an app

        Between the Lines then opens like an app, in its own window or full screen from your Home Screen, and works offline.

        Keyboard shortcuts

        Ctrl Z / Ctrl Y
        Back and Forward (undo and redo)
        Ctrl B I U
        Bold, italic, underline
        Tab / Shift Tab
        Indent or outdent a list; move between table cells and journal entry fields
        Ctrl A
        In a note: its text. Elsewhere: every note on the page
        Arrow keys
        Move selected notes (with Shift: 10 px)
        Delete
        Delete the selected notes, picture or video
        Ctrl \
        Show or hide the sidebar
        Ctrl Shift L
        Change the theme: System, Light or Dark
        Ctrl Shift F
        Focus mode on or off
        Ctrl K / Ctrl P
        Search notebooks, pages and notes, and run actions
        /
        In a note: add an equation, table or journal entry, or turn it into a sticky note
        Esc
        Close a menu or end a selection

        PowerPoint files

        How a PowerPoint (.pptx) file opens next time. Slides as they look draws each slide like a PDF page, with its text you can select; Editable text rebuilds the slides as text you can type inside.

        Custom dictionary

        Words you add here (or with “Add to dictionary” on a misspelt word) are never marked as mistakes.

          Favourite colours

          Save colours with the star next to the text colour, or Save in a pencil icon’s colour menu. They show up in both.

          Not connected

          A notebook saved to OneDrive keeps itself in sync: changes go up a few seconds after you stop writing, and changes made on another device are fetched when you come back. If Microsoft’s sign-in runs out, press the cloud button at the top to sign in again.

          Formula
          Type / for a fraction (use brackets for longer parts: (a+b)/(c+d)), ^ for a power, _ for a small low letter, sqrt(), sum. Press Enter to insert.
