        Help & settings

        How to use it

        • Open a PDF, PowerPoint (.pptx) or Word (.docx) file, or drop one anywhere on the page. Each file becomes its own notebook in the sidebar; Insert › Add a file here adds one to the notebook you're in instead.
        • On a PDF page, point at a spot and press + Add here, then pick Note (the page opens up between the lines), Sticky note or Equation. On other pages, click where you want to write. In a note, type / for the same choices.
        • Sticky notes sit on top of the page: drag one by its tape to move it, even onto another page. In the Notes panel, type a note and press Add note, or drag Sticky onto any page.
        • The Home tab styles your notes: the font, size and colour are used for new notes, or click in a note (or select words) to change just that.
        • Drag a note’s edges to resize it, or its move handle to move it. Pink lines show when it lines up with other notes; hold Alt or Shift to move it freely.
        • On each note: the arrows float it on top of the page, the minus minimises it to a pencil icon (drag the icon aside, right-click it to colour it), and the pin keeps it open.
        • The sidebar (left) lists your notebooks: click one to switch, and the one you're in shows its pages. Use its ⋯ to rename or delete it. Hide it with « (Ctrl+\) and point at the left edge to peek at it.
        • Notes (right) lists every note in the notebook to search, filter by icon colour and jump to.
        • In Chrome or Edge, Install as an app (at the bottom of the sidebar) gives Between the Slides its own window that works offline.
        • Focus hides the toolbar, side panels and status bar so only your pages and notes are left (Esc brings them back). Each panel also closes with its ×, and View › Toolbar can shrink the toolbar to tabs only.
        • Study mode stops accidental changes while you revise. Everything is saved in this browser; Download a PDF, or a notebook file to keep editing on another device.

        Keyboard shortcuts

        Ctrl Z / Ctrl Y
        Back and Forward (undo and redo)
        Ctrl B I U
        Bold, italic, underline
        Tab / Shift Tab
        Indent or outdent a list
        Ctrl A
        In a note: its text. Elsewhere: every note on the page
        Arrow keys
        Move selected notes (with Shift: 10 px)
        Delete
        Delete the selected notes, picture or video
        Ctrl \
        Show or hide the sidebar
        Ctrl Shift F
        Focus mode on or off
        /
        In a note: add an equation, or turn it into a sticky note
        Esc
        Close a menu or end a selection

        Custom dictionary

        Words you add here (or with “Add to dictionary” on a misspelt word) are never marked as mistakes.

          Favourite colours

          Save colours with the star next to the text colour, or Save in a pencil icon’s colour menu. They show up in both.

          Formula
          Type / for a fraction (use brackets for longer parts: (a+b)/(c+d)), ^ for a power, _ for a small low letter, sqrt(), sum. Press Enter to insert.
