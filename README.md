# Between the Slides

A simple web page for taking notes on lecture slides.

- Open a **PDF** and each page shows exactly as it is, with background graphics, side bands and slide numbers removed.
- Point at a spot on a page and press **+ Add note here**. The page opens up at the nearest gap between lines, and you type in your own font, size and colour.
- Notes sit in clear boxes. Drag a box's edges to resize it, or its ✥ handle to move it across the page (it snaps to the centre) or to another spot between lines, like a text box in Word.
- Choose each note box's **outline** (on/off and colour) and **fill** colour from the top bar.
- Add a **blank page** after any slide for longer notes (**+ Page after** on a slide, or **+ Blank page**), and remove pages you don't need.
- Word-style formatting in notes: undo/redo, bullet and numbered lists (Tab / Shift+Tab to indent), alignment, strikethrough, superscript/subscript, highlighter and clear formatting.
- **∑ Formula** inserts a formula into a note: type it plainly, e.g. `w_i = (P_i)/(sum_(i=1)^N P_i)` or `Q*_A`, or use the symbol buttons, with a live preview. Click a formula to edit it.
- Press a note's **⇄** button to float it on top of the page (like "in front of text" in Word), so a small note can sit beside a line without pushing anything apart. Press ⇄ again to put it back between the lines.
- Minimise a note to a small ✎ icon with its **–** button, like a comment in Word: it takes no space, shows the note when you point at it and opens when clicked, or use **Hide notes / Show notes** for all of them at once.
- Select and copy text from the PDF pages.
- **PowerPoint (.pptx)** and **Word (.docx)** files open as text with their formatting, and you can type inside sentences too.
- Pick a **page layout** (Default, A4 or A3, portrait or landscape) and each slide with its notes is shown on its own sheet of that size, shrunk to fit if needed.
- **Fewer tools** shrinks the toolbar to one row of everyday tools; **Hide toolbar** hides it (bring it back with **Show toolbar**).
- Notes are saved automatically in your browser.
- **Download** your work as a **PDF** (pages with your notes, to read or print, in the layout you picked, with notes shown on the page, as PDF comments you click to open, or left out) or as a **notebook file** (one .html file you can open later on any device, or with Open file, to keep editing).

## Use it

Open it at **https://ylp-games.github.io/Between-the-lines/**, or open `index.html` in a browser. It is a single file with no build step.

Pushing to `main` publishes the site to GitHub Pages automatically (`.github/workflows/static.yml`).
